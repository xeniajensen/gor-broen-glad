FROM THE STORYBOARD6 FRAMES → 1 SCREEN
  1. 🌧

    Frames 1-2 · trykket stemning

    Open the app after a fortnight away: storm, tears, shivering. The mood should feel like the sad face on the pylon, not like a stats screen.

  2. 🌤

    Frame 3 · den liver op

    The BroBizz beeps. White flash, clouds part, sun rays swing in, brows lift. The weather turns in about a second.

  3. 😉

    Frame 4 · et blink

    It winks at you. Once. Then a wobble and hearts - this is the child-in-the-back-seat moment.

  4. 💙

    Frames 5-6 · glad

    Blue sky, blush, chatter. Streak and level tick up quietly in the corner. Jo flere der kører, jo gladere bliver den.

All numbers are invented. Open questions before building: latency from gantry read to phone, whether care actions (nus, godnat) can carry real rewards or stay purely emotional, and how sad is too sad for a brand that people also cross in a hurry.
